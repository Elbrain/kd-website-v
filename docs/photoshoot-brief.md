# Photoshoot Brief — New Premises & Personal Training

Commission once the new premises fit-out is complete (target: 2–3 weeks before the September 2026 opening so launch marketing uses real imagery). One full day minimum: half day property, half day training. All shots needed in **both horizontal (3:2 / 16:9) and vertical (4:5 / 9:16)** for web + social. Deliverables: colour-graded, 4000px+ long edge, web-optimised exports supplied separately.

Style direction: natural light first; charcoal/neutral palette consistent with brand; energetic and warm for training, calm and architectural for property; no HDR gloss, no fisheye, no heavy filters. People shots require signed releases.
